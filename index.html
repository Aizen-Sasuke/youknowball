How to play

Each round gives you five clues about a footballer, in a random order. Guess early to score more: 5 points on the first clue, down to 1 on the last. Harder players add bonus points, and a wrong guess can cost 1. Play together on one phone, take on today's Daily, or race friends online with a room code.
